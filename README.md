# Marginloom

Evidence for when to trust an AI prediction—and when to ask a person.

An interactive workspace for exploring how reliable AI predictions are, comparing decision rules, recording human reviews and producing evidence reports.

[Live demo](https://marginloom.zhongjiehuang.com) · [GitHub](https://github.com/zhjpersonal83-cell/marginloom)

## What you can do

- Explore where a model is confident, uncertain or frequently wrong.
- Compare the trade-off between automatic decisions and human review.
- Import prediction results or try the included example models.
- Compare results across groups and inspect mistakes in more detail.
- Record review notes and save the decision settings used for an evaluation.
- Export evidence reports and explore an illustrative image-analysis viewer.

Separate workspaces keep each evaluation and its review records organised.

## A simple way to explore

Choose an example or import results, inspect the mistakes, adjust the review policy, and export the evidence behind your decision.

## Scope

A personal research and software demonstration developed with AI assistance. Included examples and the image viewer use synthetic data. They do not establish real-world safety, medical validity or production readiness. Human review notes do not change the evaluation's reference answers.
